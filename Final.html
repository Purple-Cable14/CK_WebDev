Goblin Valley

Goblin Valley is a very big place full of Hoodoos. Hoodoos are like tall rock pillars. Goblin Valley is located in Emery County Utah.

Reasons to go!

  • Looks beautiful
  • Fun to explore
  • Fun to climb Hoodoos
  • Great place to make memories with friends and family

















What You Need to know:

Hours Open 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. daily. Camping Camp in designated areas only, with a limit of one vehicle and up to eight people per site; additional vehicles require an extra fee. Check-in is at 3:00 p.m., check-out is at noon, and the maximum stay is 14 nights. Quiet Hours Quiet hours, including for generators, run from 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.. Fires Campfires are restricted to designated rings, fireworks and shooting are prohibited, and importing out-of-state or Canadian firewood is banned unless UDAF heat-treated and certified. Pets Pets must be kept on a leash no longer than 6 feet and cleaned up after promptly. Bicycles and Vehicles Bikes are permitted only on paved roads and approved trails near Curtis Bench Trail, and are banned on hiking trails, unpaved campground areas, and the Valley of Goblins. Vehicles must stay on designated roads and campsites, with no cross-country driving allowed. Plants, Animals, and Formations All natural features and wildlife are protected; removing, altering, destroying, digging, collecting firewood, or harassing wildlife is unlawful. Visitors must pack out all trash. Drones Year-round drone flights require a $15 daily permit from the Visitor Center and are restricted to Valley 1 from the Observation Point platform, with no flights over occupied dispersed campsites. Weekend and holiday flights are banned from March 1 through October 31, and weekday permits are capped at 2 per hour and 10 per day. Rappelling Rappelling into Goblin's Lair requires a $4 daily backcountry permit from the Visitor Center.

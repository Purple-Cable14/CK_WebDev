Ninjago

In the Beginning

In the very beginning, there was only one realm; The Realm of the Oni and the Dragon, which was inhabited by Oni and Dragons. The Oni had the power of destruction, while the dragons had the power of creation. Since these two powers were complete opposites, a war broke out between the two. Amidst the war a child was born of both the power of the Oni and the Dragons. The Oni and the Dragons both wanted to take and use the child for their gain in the war. The child made dragon armor and tried to stop the war with a dragon named Firstbourne, however neither the Dragons, nor the Oni would admit defeat. Seeing no other option, the child created The Realm Crystal, which would allow the user to travel through realms. After travelling through many realms, he found a realm of endless ocean, with very few islands.

Before the Creation of Ninjago

When the boy born from Oni and Dragon made his way to a realm of endless ocean, he found that it was ruled by a giant sea serpent named Wojira who could control the power of the wind and water. Wojira had two amulets on its head, each amulet containing each element. With the help of the Islanders, the Merlopians, and Nyad the first elemental master of water. The only way Wojira could be defeated, was when Nyad merged with the endless sea to take Wojira down. In this process Nyad forgot who she was, and was now part of the ocean. The boy gave the islanders the storm amulet, which gave Wojira the wind powers, and he gave the merlopians the wave amulet, which gave Wojira the water powers. Wojira would be in a deep slumber until the amulets return to it. During the boys time in the endless ocean, he built the temple of light, which he later used to make the four golden weapons of creation from the golden peaks.

Quick breakdown of Ninjago

There has been about 20 seasons of Ninjago includeing the pilots, and discludeing the other special/mini seasons/specials. These seasons have been running from january of 2011 to present day, so its been on for over 15 years. The first 2 seasons were intended to be the only seasons, but the fans of ninjago, so Wilfilm made a rebooted season, followed by many more to come. The first ten seasons were made by Wilfilm, and they had their own ending with the Oni trilogy, which was the last 3 seasons of the first 10, and this trilogy is considered some of the best, or some of the worst depending on who you ask. 5 more seasons were made by seperate group, which had their own ending, which referenced many allies made through the first 15 seasons. Currently there is one more set of seasons called Dragons Rising, which has 4 seasons currently and is still going. In the long run, Ninjago has being ever-growing and evolveing, and I doubt it will end any time soon because Ninjago is going to make an anime call Ninjago Legends.
